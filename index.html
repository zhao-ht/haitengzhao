Bio

Welcome! I am a researcher at Shanghai AI Lab, working on advanced reasoning, multimodal foundation models, autonomous agents, and AI for science.

I received my Ph.D. in Machine Learning and Intelligence from Peking University in 2025, where I also earned my B.S. in Psychology. Over the course of my research, I have gained hands-on experience contributing to trillion-scale foundation models, reinforcement learning systems, and advanced reasoning agents. My work has led to over 20 papers and preprints, with publications in top-tier venues such as ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, ACL, EMNLP, COLM, and CVPR.

My research is broadly driven by the question of whether deep learning can effectively encode human-like intelligence. Currently, I focus on three main areas:

  1. Advanced Reasoning: Addressing compositional generalization challenges in deep models, which are crucial for achieving human-like intelligence. My research in this area mainly focuses on long-horizon reasoning and tackling highly complex mathematical challenges. Specifically, I explore how to scale the length and complexity of reasoning steps, and how to enable these reasoning capabilities to self-evolve during training.
  2. AI for Science: I am deeply interested in the potential of AI to drive scientific discovery. While today's AI demonstrates strong general capabilities, enabling it to autonomously conduct effective scientific research—reasoning through experiments and generating new insights—remains an open question. I bridge this gap by developing scientific multimodal foundation models.
  3. Foundations of Large Language Models: Investigating core topics such as foundation model architectures, generalization theory, and robustness. As large language models demonstrate impressive general capabilities, there is an urgent need to establish foundational theories and scalable architectures. In particular, my work focuses on improving current LLM frameworks and training paradigms, alongside developing theoretical tools to better predict and analyze their capabilities.

I am always happy to connect and collaborate with you. Feel free to reach out via email!
